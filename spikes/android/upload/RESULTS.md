Decision: **GO** for integrating native URL/session renewal and API34+ UIDT recovery in the production uploader (SA-57), conditional on the pending gates. Native control-plane transients still require explicit Resume in this prototype; no unlimited background execution is promised. The fixture, harness and APKs are test-only: nothing here is deployed to Render, added to a Blueprint or reachable by production accounts.

Runtime evidence is the emulator run recorded in [emulator-run-result.json](emulator-run-result.json); it contains hashes, counters and journals only (no cookies or signed URLs). The emulator was stopped afterward.

## CI wiring (2026-11-01)

`android-upload-spike-native:build` (`app/project.json`) now runs in the pinned Android CI job whenever spike sources change: `assembleDebug`, `assembleDebugAndroidTest` and `lintDebug`. Locally it was verified with default executable discovery and again from an empty `GRADLE_USER_HOME` with `CI=true` and no `SPIKE_GRADLE_BIN` (Gradle 8.14 bootstrapped by `apps/mobile/android/gradlew`; 67 tasks executed; lint 0 errors / 24 warnings). The rebuilt APKs differ byte-for-byte from the hashes in `build-result.json`, which still identify the exact emulator-run inputs. No new emulator or device run was performed for this CI-only change; the earlier emulator receipt is unchanged.
